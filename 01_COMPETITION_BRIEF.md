# InnovateX 2026–27 — Requirements from the Supplied PDF

**Source of truth:** `source/InnovateX_Techfest_2026-27_Brief.pdf` (9 pages). This Markdown is a working summary; if any wording matters for eligibility or compliance, reopen the PDF.

## Chosen theme: Theme 1 — Underground Water Management

The brief describes IoT-based underground-water management around connected sensors, monitoring systems and analytics for groundwater depletion, contamination, illegal extraction, pipeline leaks and uncertain recharge.

The challenge asks for an IoT solution to monitor, protect and manage underground water resources in an urban city. Its examples include water-level monitoring, contamination detection, illegal extraction prevention, pipeline leakage detection and aquifer recharge analysis.

## What the brief expects teams to address

1. A specific groundwater problem, not a general-purpose smart-city platform.
2. An IoT-based solution with sensors, communications and analytics.
3. Technical feasibility: sensor operation, data collection, networking and real-time monitoring.
4. Operational challenges: underground connectivity, durability, power, maintenance and data reliability.
5. Economic feasibility and a model for long-term adoption.
6. Environmental and social impact.

## Round 1 format from the PDF

- Physical pitch at the relevant regional/zonal centre.
- Ten minutes per team.
- The submission should cover team information, precise problem statement, background/context and existing solutions, proposed solution/technology, impact and scalability.
- The PDF does not mandate exactly five slides. **Five slides are a recommended communication format for this pack**, not an official rule.
- The brief says that the Top five at each regional zonal centre qualify for Round 2.

## Round 2 expectations and dates listed in the PDF

The PDF says teams qualifying for Round 2 should build a prototype. It lists a final report due **1 December 2026** and final presentations at Techfest, IIT Bombay, **15–18 December 2026**. Reconfirm event details on the official portal before relying on them.

## Evaluation criteria

The PDF lists creativity/novelty, functionality/usability, cost/market value, technical implementation/feasibility, regional scalability, environmental/socioeconomic impact, design/ergonomics and a bonus for detailed, region-specific problem understanding.

## Guidance for this project's deck

- Tie every major claim to the brief or an identifiable source.
- Show technical feasibility, architecture, cost ranges and operational risks.
- Explain where hardware is proposed versus currently demonstrated.
- Show one observable end-to-end virtual scenario.
- Make Bengaluru the initial context and explain how the data model could adapt across India.
- Do not invent official requirements or state a five-slide deck is compulsory.
