## Evidence labels used throughout the reports

- **Verified:** the cited source explicitly states the capability or rule.
- **Not found in reviewed material:** the reviewed source set did not show the feature. This is not proof that a product lacks it.
- **Confirmed unavailable:** used only when a source explicitly says the feature is unavailable. No major competitor received this label in the current scan.
- **Vendor claim:** first-party product marketing, not an independently measured result.
- **Plan / target:** a reported or announced future deployment, not an achieved deployment.
- **Unverified / illustrative:** a simulation or assumption that must not be presented as field data.
