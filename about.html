LIMITATIONS

What this does not prove

  • It is not connected to a real borewell or tanker.
  • A quantity mismatch is not proof of theft, fraud or illegal extraction.
  • GPS/QR is an identifier, not source legitimacy.
  • Flow measurement does not prove potability.
  • ₹0 demo cost is not ₹0 deployment cost.
  • Market gap and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.
FIELD VALIDATION

Smallest next experiment

  1. Talk to five apartment/facility managers and suppliers.
  2. Map their current source, load and delivery records.
  3. Confirm NOC/permit evidence and who can access it.
  4. Run a reference-meter comparison before claiming accuracy.
  5. Measure linkage rate, false alerts and time-to-review.

Engineering stance: ordinary, explainable rules are enough for the first prototype. Add ML only if future labelled field data shows it improves a specific decision.
